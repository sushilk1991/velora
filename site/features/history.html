Velora/Features/History & recovery

Feature

Nothing is
thrown away.

Cleanup is a model, and models are sometimes wrong. So every dictation keeps its raw transcript alongside the polished result — searchable, replayable, and re-processable on your Mac, for as long as you choose.

Download for Mac ↓ Install options

What’s kept

A paper trail you control.

Raw + final

Both versions, always

What the speech model heard and what cleanup produced sit side by side. If polish went wrong, the original is one click away — not gone.

Audio too

Replay the moment

Dictation audio is archived locally as compressed FLAC. Play it back when the transcript alone can’t settle what you actually said.

Reprocess

Second chances for old takes

Re-run any past dictation through a different mode or an upgraded model. Yesterday’s meeting ramble can become today’s clean notes.

Fast at scale

Built for years of use

Search and retention are benchmarked against a 100,000-row local history — the app stays instant even when your archive isn’t small.

The fine print

  • History lives only on your Mac — it is as backed up as your Mac is. Time Machine covers it; Velora does not phone it anywhere.
  • Clear All means it: every saved transcript and archived audio clip is permanently deleted.
  • Retention is configurable; bulk selection and structured export are still on the roadmap.
