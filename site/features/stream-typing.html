Velora/Features/Stream Typing

Feature · new

Speak now.
Fix it while you talk.

Press ⌃⇧S and a live draft appears at your cursor as you speak. Say “3 p.m. — no, 6 p.m.” and watch the draft correct itself, then settle into polished text.

Download for Mac ↓ Install options

Why it feels different

Live text, without live risk.

Most live dictation buys immediacy by gambling with your document. Stream Typing is engineered so the worst case is always “the final text is on your clipboard” — never “it mangled my paragraph.”

Owned drafts

It only rewrites its own words

Every provisional update proves it is still in the same app, the same text field, the same range, with the same draft it wrote — before touching anything. Your surrounding text is never in play.

You always win

Type, and it backs off

Press any real key or click anywhere and Velora immediately stops rewriting. The finished text is already staged on your clipboard before the last replacement, so nothing is ever lost.

Terminals

Honest about opaque apps

Known terminals expose no readable text, so Velora shows the live draft in its own HUD and inserts one polished final when you finish — it never fires blind backspaces at a shell.

Opt-in

Normal dictation stays fast

Stream Typing is its own shortcut. Ordinary hold-to-talk keeps the lower-latency final-only path — you choose the feel per dictation, not in a settings maze.

The fine print

  • Live in-field drafts need an exact accessibility target; in known terminals the draft lives in Velora’s HUD, then inserts once at the end.
  • If focus, cursor, text, or secure input changes mid-stream, Velora stops rewriting and keeps the final on the clipboard.
  • Stream Typing and Action Mode never post keyboard events at the same time.
  • Non-input surfaces are unavailable by design.
