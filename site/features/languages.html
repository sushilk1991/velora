Velora/Features/Languages & models

Feature

99 languages.
Zero uploads.

The default speech model — Whisper large-v3-turbo — covers 99 languages including Hindi, Indian English, Mandarin, Arabic, Spanish, and French. Set a language, or let Velora detect it mid-sentence.

The model menu

Pick your trade-off. They’re all local.

Every option downloads once and runs on your Mac. Velora defaults to the best all-rounder and lets you trade accuracy, speed, and disk when your needs are sharper.

Default · 1.6 GB

Whisper large-v3-turbo

Fast and multilingual — 99 languages with the best all-round balance of speed and accuracy. What most people should use, so it’s what ships selected.

Accuracy · 3.1 GB

Whisper large-v3

The full-size model: same languages, more headroom on hard accents and noisy rooms. Slower and larger — a deliberate trade.

Hinglish · 1.6 GB

Hindi specialist

Fine-tuned on 700+ hours of Hindi–English code-switching, with Romanized output. Built for the way Hinglish is actually spoken, not textbook Hindi.

Speed · 2.3–2.5 GB

Parakeet v2 & v3

The lowest-latency finals: v2 for pure English, v3 adding 24 European languages. No Hindi, Mandarin, or Arabic — pure speed for the languages they cover.

Small · 0.5 GB

Turbo, 4-bit

The smallest multilingual option for tight disks — roughest quality, still 99 languages, still local.

Cleanup tiers

Qwen, sized to your RAM

The writing-cleanup model comes in tiers matched to 8, 16, and 24 GB+ Macs — Velora picks the right one for your machine automatically.

The fine print

  • Language auto-detection is the Whisper family’s; the Parakeet models are English/European-first by design.
  • Model quality claims here come from Velora’s own saved-audio benchmarks in the repo — run them yourself if you’re curious.
  • Switching models re-downloads once; nothing is streamed at dictation time.
