Velora/Features/Personal dictionary

Feature

It learns
your nouns.

Every field has its Priyas, Kubernetes-es, and product codenames that generic speech models mangle. Velora keeps a personal dictionary that fixes them — and learns new ones from the corrections you make.

Download for Mac ↓ Install options

How it learns

Corrections in, accuracy out.

Vocabulary

Terms the model should trust

Add names, brands, and jargon once, and the speech model is nudged toward them on every dictation. Validated on entry, so a typo never becomes a permanent bias.

Replacements

Always-wrong, auto-fixed

When a term is transcribed wrong the same way every time, a replacement rule rewrites it after the fact — deterministic, visible, and editable.

Learning

Your edits teach it

Velora mines the corrections you actually make and proposes dictionary entries with a visible receipt — you confirm what it learned; it never silently rewires itself.

Portable

CSV in, CSV out

Bring a term list from anywhere, export yours anytime. Optional sync uses your own iCloud Drive and carries confirmed terms only — never audio, never transcripts.

The fine print

  • The Whisper speech models consume the dictionary during transcription; the Parakeet models do not yet — their replacements still apply after the fact.
  • Sync is off by default. Turning it on uses your iCloud Drive, not a Velora server — there isn’t one.
