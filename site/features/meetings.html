Velora/Features/Meeting notes

Feature

Records the room.
Never the screen.

After you confirm — every time — Velora captures your microphone and your Mac’s audio in two tracks, keeps a visible recording indicator running, and turns the meeting into a transcript and notes you can search. All of it stays on your Mac.

Download for Mac ↓ Install options

What you get

A meeting memory you actually own.

Consent first

No silent recording, ever

Every capture starts with an explicit confirmation and runs with a persistent recording indicator. There is no auto-record setting to forget about — that is a design decision, not a missing feature.

Two tracks

Your mic and the room

Microphone and system audio are captured separately, so your voice and the other side stay distinct. Speaker separation labels who said what — without guessing at anyone’s identity.

Local processing

Transcript, notes, search

The same on-device models produce a transcript and structured notes. Processing is resumable — a long meeting keeps its progress — and everything is searchable, replayable, exportable, and deletable from History.

Knows when it’s over

Ends with the meeting

When the meeting app releases your microphone, Velora offers to wrap up — scoped to the capture you approved, so it never lingers past the call.

The fine print

  • Velora captures audio only — never your screen, slides, or camera.
  • Call audio keeps playing during capture; meeting recording does not pause anyone’s playback.
  • Recording other people may require their consent where you live — the confirmation step is there for a reason.
  • Deleting a meeting from History permanently deletes its transcript and archived audio.
