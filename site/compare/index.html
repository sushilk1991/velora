Credit where due

What the others do better.

A comparison you can trust has to cut both ways. If your needs match these, use the other tool — genuinely.

Wispr Flow

Reach and team polish

Native apps on Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android, enterprise compliance (SOC 2, HIPAA-ready), and a very polished onboarding. If you need dictation on Windows or a managed team rollout, Velora simply doesn’t do that.

Superwhisper

Depth of configuration

A longer track record, a bigger model catalogue, per-mode shortcuts and paste policies, deep links, and iOS + Windows apps. Power users who love tuning will find more dials there today.

FluidVoice

Hacker energy

Open source like Velora, with multiple local engines and speaker-labelled file transcription. Its voice-triggered shell commands are powerful — Velora deliberately refuses that trade for a safer action model.

Apple Dictation

Zero setup

It’s already on your Mac and works offline for supported languages. If you dictate a sentence a week and don’t mind cleaning it up yourself, you may not need anything else.
