Velora/Compare/vs Wispr Flow

Compare · checked August 2026

Velora vs
Wispr Flow.

Wispr Flow is probably the most polished dictation product on the market. The comparison isn’t about polish — it’s about architecture: every Wispr Flow dictation is processed on their servers, and every Velora dictation is processed on your Mac.

Download for Mac ↓ Install options

The core difference

Their words, not ours.

That is a legitimate engineering choice — and it means your audio, your transcripts, and context from the app you’re typing in travel to their servers on every dictation. Privacy mode reduces retention and opts out of model training; it does not change where the processing happens. Velora’s answer is structural: the models live on your disk, so there is nowhere to send your voice.

Head to head

Where each one wins.

Choose Velora if you want

  • Dictation that works with the network cable unplugged — audio and transcripts never leave your Mac.
  • Free forever: no $12–15/month, no 2,000-words-per-week meter on the free tier.
  • No account, no sign-in, nothing to be breached upstream.
  • Open source you can read, build, and verify — MIT-licensed.
  • Local extras no cloud tool matches: consent-first meeting notes, Safe Voice Edit, and Action Mode driving real apps.
  • Sensitive work — legal, medical, unreleased code — where “processed on our servers” is a non-starter regardless of policy.

Choose Wispr Flow if you need

  • Windows, iPhone, or Android — Velora is Mac-only (Apple Silicon), full stop.
  • A managed team rollout: SSO, admin controls, SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA agreements.
  • Zero local compute: their cloud does the work, so an older or busy machine stays free.
  • Their cross-device consistency — the same tool on your phone and your laptop.

Pricing checked August 2026: Wispr Flow Pro is $12/user/month billed annually or $15 monthly; the free tier allows about 2,000 words/week on desktop. Velora is free.
