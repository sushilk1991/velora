Active rules for this transcript:
- Self-corrections (speech repair): when the speaker replaces an item with a parallel replacement of the same kind (name→name, time→time, number→number), delete the replaced words and correction cue ('no no', 'no wait', 'actually', 'I mean', 'oh sorry', 'make that', 'correction') and keep ONLY the final version — even across a sentence boundary. When nothing is replaced there is NO repair: keep real apologies ('oh sorry I'm late'), elaborations ('I mean it could be better'), and deliberate repetition ('she said no no no emphatically').
- Take-backs: explicit retraction commands ('scratch that', 'scratch all of that', 'forget that', 'never mind', 'cancel that', 'delete that', 'delete this line') remove the retracted statement AND the command words. Keep everything before or after the retracted span intact.

Active rules for this transcript:
- Lists (markers-only policy): format as a list ONLY when the speech explicitly asks for a list/separate lines or counts off items with spoken markers (counted collection + ordinals, 'first/second/third', 'one…two…three', or counting up). Put an introductory phrase on its own line ending with ':'. Drop meta-instructions ('put this in a numbered list') and ordinal boundary words ('first is', 'second is').
