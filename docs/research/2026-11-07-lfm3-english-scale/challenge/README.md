# Public heldout challenge

`fresh-active.jsonl` contains 256 active synthetic text cases; `fresh-bypass.jsonl` contains 52 cases reserved for bypass checks. `fresh-guards.jsonl` contains 312 guard cases, including those 308 cases and four rejected cases retained for exclusion. The files and `fresh-freeze.json` are frozen public artifacts. Their author and model-assisted review are recorded in the freeze manifest; the review is not human, audio, or accent certification. Regional labels represent written-language metadata, not evidence of speaker origin.

`training-exclusions-v5.jsonl` is the 443-case pre-existing guard from the repository. The complete frozen guard union had 779 cases: those 443, the 312 synthetic guards, and 24 acoustic cases. This directory does not reproduce the complete union, because its acoustic guard records include recognition outputs; those outputs and audio are not published here. Do not infer that the 443 + 312 published text records alone reproduce all checks performed against the 779-case union.

`accent-case-references.jsonl` identifies the 24 public source cases without transcripts, audio, or model recognition outputs. They are from 12 speakers in six corpus-supplied accent groups. The source is the [CSTR VCTK Corpus 0.92](https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2645), published by the University of Edinburgh under CC BY 4.0; the official item, archive, readme and licence URLs are recorded in `accent-audio-acquisition.json`. Corpus transcripts were not manually verified for this experiment. These read studio sentences are not representative of spontaneous speech, and the source's accent categories are not independent accent assessments. `accent-freeze.json` preserves the historical freeze record and hashes; a hash of an excluded recognition artifact is not the artifact itself.

The challenge and acoustic cases are held out for evaluation. Do not use them for training or checkpoint selection. Their use does not establish broad real-world or accent performance.
