# Velora 0.21.3

Proofread and Voice Edit now work on text selected in Chrome and other apps
that hide their selection from macOS.

- Fixes “Select some text first” on web pages in Chrome, including YouTube.
- Reads the selection by asking the app to copy it, then puts your
  clipboard back.
- Pastes the result over the selection when it sits in an editable field;
  on a static page the result stays on the clipboard.
- Keeps your clipboard intact when a proofread follows a dictation closely.
- Leaves the result on the clipboard instead of pasting when you switch
  apps or click elsewhere before it arrives.

## Upgrade

Replace the existing app in Applications with the new DMG, or update
in-app. Preferences, history, modes, and dictionary entries are
preserved.
