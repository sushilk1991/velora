# Velora 0.35.0

- Adds a customizable Dictate & Send shortcut (`⌃⇧↩` by default) in Settings › Shortcuts and Home. Hold it to see your words live, then release to paste and press Return automatically.
- Upgrades live speech preview to a floating Live Transcript Card showing up to three lines of live text, an inline waveform, the active app and mode, and a clear Release to Send indicator.
- Adds a Recording view picker (Compact Pill or Live Card) in Settings › General and a Send key choice (`↩ Return` or `⌘↩ Cmd+Return`). Mail, Outlook, and Superhuman stay paste-only by default so email drafts do not send accidentally.
