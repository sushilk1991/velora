# Velora 0.21.4

Proofread now pastes its result in Chrome and applies your dictionary
spellings.

- Fixes “Selection changed — edit on clipboard” when you hold the proofread
  shortcut a moment too long; the result now replaces the selection.
- Proofread and Voice Edit correct misspellings of names and terms in your
  dictionary, including your own name.
- Names that merely resemble a dictionary entry are left as you typed them.
- Automatically learned terms are not applied to text you typed, only the
  entries you added or corrected yourself.

## Upgrade

Replace the existing app in Applications with the new DMG, or update
in-app. Preferences, history, modes, and dictionary entries are
preserved.
