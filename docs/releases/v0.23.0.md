# Velora 0.23.0

Velora is one app window now, not a settings screen: Home, History,
Stats, Meetings, Dictionary and Modes behind a floating sidebar, in the
macOS 26 look.

- Home shows today’s words, dictations, speaking time and time saved,
  your recent dictations, the Ways to Talk toggles and the latest
  meeting in one place.
- History reads as a journal: day by day, one entry expanded at a time,
  with copy, paste again, play, reprocess and delete on the entry.
- Settings is a compact ⌘, window with five tabs: General, Dictation,
  Shortcuts, Models and Advanced. Export/Import and the meeting extras
  live under Advanced.
- Closing the window keeps the pill and the menubar item. Show Pill in
  the menubar and Close Pill on the pill turn it off and on; a pill
  closed mid-dictation comes back when you turn it on again.
- New colours: warm neutrals with a sky accent in both light and dark,
  plus a refreshed icon. The website matches.
- Removed: the volume slider, the pill Position menu, the typing-speed
  stepper, the menubar Recent Transcriptions list (still on the pill’s
  right-click menu and in History) and the Setup Assistant menubar
  entry (still under Help and Settings › Advanced).

## Upgrade

Replace the existing app in Applications with the new DMG, or update
in-app. Preferences, history, modes, and dictionary entries are
preserved.
