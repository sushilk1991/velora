# Velora 0.16.3

Stream Typing lets you see your words while you speak. Press ⌃⇧S, talk
naturally, and watch the draft follow along, including mid-sentence corrections.
When you finish, Velora leaves clean text where you were writing.

- Editors update the draft at your cursor.
- Terminals show the live draft in Velora, then insert it when you finish.
- Finder can now send audio and video files straight to Velora for transcription.
- Meetings recover more reliably from interrupted capture and processing.
