# Velora 0.20.0

A consistency pass over the whole Settings window, plus your dictionary
now shows what Velora just learned.

- **One background everywhere.** Every pane — General, Dictation,
  Dictionary, Models, Modes, History, Stats, Meetings, Shortcuts,
  About — now sits on the same canvas in both light and dark mode. The
  Shortcuts and Stats pages no longer look darker than the rest, and
  the titlebar, sidebar, and content read as a single surface.
- **The window has a title again.** The Settings titlebar shows the
  current page name — one title, in the native spot, instead of a
  bare titlebar over a duplicate header.
- **Newly learned words surface first.** The Dictionary list is sorted
  newest-first, so terms Velora just picked up from your dictations
  appear at the top instead of hiding in an alphabetical middle. Use
  search to look up a specific entry. Also fixed the open Dictionary
  page lagging one change behind the list it displays.
- **Cleaner shortcut recording.** The Right Option / Fn / F19
  quick-pick buttons are gone from Settings — click the shortcut and
  press the keys you want. (First-run onboarding keeps the
  suggestions.)
- The voice-edit card once again lists "fix the grammar" among its
  example commands.

## Upgrade

Replace the existing app in Applications with the new DMG, or update
in-app. Preferences, history, modes, and dictionary entries are
preserved.
