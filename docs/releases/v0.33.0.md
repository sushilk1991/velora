# Velora 0.33.0

- Wakes the writing model as soon as you press the dictation shortcut and trims leading and trailing silence, cutting peak writing-model memory by 476 MB while keeping prose intact unless you speak explicit list markers.
- Converts spoken numbers, times, dates, and currencies to digits and matches saved dictionary names phonetically even when speech recognition spells them differently.
- Switches automatically when a dead built-in microphone is selected with the MacBook lid closed, settles newly connected Bluetooth headsets before recording, and follows macOS default-input changes mid-dictation.
- Lowers playback volume instead of pausing when your microphone and audio output share a Bluetooth headset, and never pauses and ducks at the same time.
- Plays distinct sound cues when you release the dictation key and when a recording contains no speech.
