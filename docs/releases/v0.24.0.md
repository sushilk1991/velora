# Velora 0.24.0

Velora now reads the words already on your screen so it can spell what
you say: names, product names, ticket IDs, the odd acronym your team
invented last month.

- Before each dictation Velora gathers a short list of terms near your
  cursor, and falls back to the active window when the text near the
  cursor is thin. Those terms become spelling hints, so a name you can
  see gets typed the way it is written rather than the way it sounds.
- With Screen Recording permission, Apple Vision reads sparse windows
  on this Mac. Screenshots are discarded after recognition; the
  recognised text and the spelling hints are never saved.
- Dictation never waits on it. If the read is slow, the words land
  anyway and the hints sit out that round.
- Onboarding says plainly what stays on this Mac. There is no Velora
  dictation server: your audio, your screenshots and the text read off
  your screen never leave the machine.
- Settings › Dictation has a “Read on-screen text for spelling” switch.
  Off stops every dictation screen-text read. Velora still uses the app
  and page you are in to pick the writing mode and to resolve spoken @
  tags.

## Upgrade

Replace the existing app in Applications with the new DMG, or update
in-app. Preferences, history, modes, and dictionary entries are
preserved.
