# Velora 0.21.0

One new shortcut, and it needs no microphone.

- **Fix spelling and grammar (⌃⇧G).** Select text in any app and press
  Control-Shift-G: Velora runs your local writing model over exactly
  what you selected — punctuation, spelling, grammar — and replaces the
  selection in place. In read-only text it copies the corrected version
  to the clipboard instead. No dictation, no speech model, nothing
  leaves your Mac. Toggle and re-record the shortcut in Settings →
  Shortcuts, in the new "Fix spelling and grammar" card.

## Upgrade

Replace the existing app in Applications with the new DMG, or update
in-app. Preferences, history, modes, and dictionary entries are
preserved.
