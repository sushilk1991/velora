# Velora 0.29.7

Velora no longer adds words you didn't say, such as a stray "Thank you." at
the end of a dictation.

- A dictation of about 30 seconds that ended in a pause could finish with
  "Thank you." that you never said. Velora now drops it. A "thank you" you
  actually say stays.
- A take where nobody spoke, in a quiet room, no longer turns into invented
  text such as "Thank you." or "Okay."
- The word "Glossary" no longer appears in your text in place of a word you
  said.
- Words Velora learns from your dictations no longer keep a misheard spelling
  of a word in your Dictionary, such as "Valora" when your Dictionary has
  "Velora". Misheard spellings it already learned stop affecting recognition.
- Action Mode opens web pages and Google searches ("open YouTube", "search
  Google for flights to Goa") at once, in your default browser.
- Speech recognition and writing cleanup still run on this Mac. Preferences,
  history, modes, and dictionary entries are preserved.
