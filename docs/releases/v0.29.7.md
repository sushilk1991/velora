# Velora 0.29.7

Cleaner dictation: Velora no longer adds words you didn't say.

- No more stray "Thank you." at the end of a dictation.
- Takes where nobody spoke no longer turn into made-up text.
- The word "Glossary" no longer appears in place of words you said.
- Velora keeps the spellings in your Dictionary instead of learning misheard
  ones.
- Action Mode opens websites and Google searches right away.
- Speech recognition and writing cleanup still run on this Mac. Preferences,
  history, modes, and dictionary entries are preserved.
