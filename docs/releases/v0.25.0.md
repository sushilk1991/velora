# Velora 0.25.0

Meeting notes now work with more microphones and still come out when
one side of a call fails, Velora looks and behaves more like a Mac app,
and short dictations are ready sooner.

## Meetings

- Microphones that record 16-bit, 24-bit or 64-bit audio, including
  many USB mics, no longer fail the whole meeting. If one side of a
  call can't be read, Velora writes notes from the other side and
  tells you which side is missing. If a recording can't be read for a
  moment, Velora tries it again a little later instead of dropping it.
- If your microphone records nothing but silence, Velora warns you
  once, a few seconds in, so you can switch inputs. A silent track no
  longer turns into lines you never said, like “Thank you.”
- When the notes model stops partway through a long meeting, the notes
  it already wrote are kept and marked partial. Retry Notes finishes
  the job.
- Choosing Not Now on a meeting suggestion now holds for the whole
  call.
- Meetings is a plain list. Click a meeting to open it, and use
  All Meetings or ⌘[ to go back.

## Design

- The window buttons sit where macOS puts them, level with the page
  title, and every page uses the same card style.
- Stats is rebuilt with charts: words per day against your average,
  when you dictate by hour and weekday, which apps you dictate into,
  and your active days, for Today, 7 Days, 30 Days or All Time.
- Home shows your last 7 days at a glance.
- Right-clicking the pill shows one short menu: Start Dictation,
  Microphone, Recent, Open Velora and Hide Pill. Settings › General
  sets when the pill shows: Always, While Dictating or Never. Quit
  stays in the menu bar menu, and in the app menu while Velora’s
  window is open.
- Speech models have plain names, like Whisper Turbo and Parakeet v3.

## Speed

- Dictation now reads each stretch of audio once instead of two or
  three times. With Whisper Turbo, the default, dictations under 10
  seconds are ready about 40% sooner.
- Everything still runs on this Mac.

## Upgrade

Replace the existing app in Applications with the new DMG, or update
in-app. Preferences, history, modes, and dictionary entries are
preserved.
