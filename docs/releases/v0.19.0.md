# Velora 0.19.0

A ground-up visual refresh — Settings now looks and feels like a native
macOS app.

- **Stats is a real dashboard.** Words, dictations, speaking time, and
  time saved at a glance; a streak card with a 12-week activity heatmap;
  a daily words chart with your average line (hover any bar for the
  exact day); and where-you-dictate breakdowns for apps and modes.
- **Shortcuts got a card per feature.** Dictation, stream typing, voice
  edit, and voice actions each show their toggle and shortcut together,
  with key-cap style shortcut display and recording.
- The Music permission row now appears only when there is something to
  do — grant access, fix a denial, or open Music — instead of showing a
  permanent "Allowed" badge.
- The sidebar highlights the selected page in your accent color, and
  each page header carries its icon.
- Streaks and the heatmap now display correctly under every language,
  calendar, and number-format setting.

## Upgrade

Replace the existing app in Applications with the new DMG, or update
in-app. Preferences, history, modes, and dictionary entries are
preserved.
