# Velora 0.23.1

The one-window app settles in: History reads as one column, Home’s
cards line up, and updates ask once and then get out of the way.

- History starts at the title’s edge instead of floating in the middle
  of the window. Rows highlight on hover, and an open entry shows the
  text and its actions with “As heard” tucked behind a disclosure.
- Home’s Recent, Ways to talk and Meetings cards share one header
  style, and Meetings shows what it does before you press Start.
- The dark window carries more of the sky and apricot tint.
- Main, Settings and About windows now share the same chrome, so the
  traffic lights and sidebar sit in the same place in each.
- Updates: the window appears at most once a day per version, Skip This
  Version hides a release until the next one ships, and closing the
  window changes nothing. With automatic updates on, Velora downloads
  and verifies quietly and offers Restart to Update when it is ready.
- Check for Updates in the menubar opens Settings › General and reports
  there.

## Upgrade

Replace the existing app in Applications with the new DMG, or update
in-app. Preferences, history, modes, and dictionary entries are
preserved.
