# Velora 0.24.2

Action Mode now opens the app you name in about a third of a second
instead of nearly three.

- Say “Open Calculator”, “switch to Slack” or “open the App Store” and
  the app comes up straight away. A command that asks for nothing but
  an app is now settled with a quick check on the local model instead
  of a full written plan.
- Anything more than opening an app, such as “open the general channel
  in Slack” or “draft a reply to Sam”, runs exactly as before.
- The name has to be the app’s own. “Chrome” opens Google Chrome, but
  “Phone” won’t open iPhone Mirroring and “Calendar” won’t open Notion
  Calendar. If a button on screen carries the name you said, like
  Slack’s Home, Velora works it out the usual way.
- The faster path runs with the Quality writing model (Qwen3.5-4B,
  8-bit), the default on Macs with 24 GB or more. The Balanced and
  Compact models keep the usual path for now.
- Everything still runs on this Mac.

## Upgrade

Replace the existing app in Applications with the new DMG, or update
in-app. Preferences, history, modes, and dictionary entries are
preserved.
