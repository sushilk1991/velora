# Velora 0.34.0

- Folds Settings into the main Velora window under one sidebar, with a horizontal section bar for General, Dictation, Shortcuts, Models, and Advanced.
- Archives finished dictations as Ogg Opus (~3× smaller than FLAC) with native playback in History, and stabilizes Stream Typing previews so trailing words stop flickering.
- Adds Whisper Tiny (English, 0.08 GB) and Whisper Base (Multilingual, 0.15 GB) as smallest-download speech options.
- Recovers automatically if computer audio drops mid-meeting, holds call-end detection across brief mutes or call switches, saves periodic recovery checkpoints, and condenses the middle of long meetings so opening context and closing action items stay intact.
- Adds a live microphone check during onboarding and one-click microphone exclusion in the pill's Microphone menu.
