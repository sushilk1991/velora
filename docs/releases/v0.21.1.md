# Velora 0.21.1

Less work during long recordings and when your Mac is short on memory.

- Reduces repeated audio analysis during longer dictations.
- Pauses background vocabulary learning when memory is under pressure,
  then resumes when your Mac has room again.
- Responds to cancellation while preparing text cleanup and releases
  memory held by the cancelled request.

The speech and writing models stay the same.

## Upgrade

Replace the existing app in Applications with the new DMG, or update
in-app. Preferences, history, modes, and dictionary entries are
preserved.
